Mounir Messaoudi

PhD candidate · SAIRPICO team, Inria · University of Rennes, France

I am a PhD candidate in the SAIRPICO team at Inria, University of Rennes, advised by Charles Kervrann. I develop deep learning methods for biomedical image analysis that remain reliable on noisy, heterogeneous data, with a focus on cryo-electron tomography (cryo-ET).

My work covers neural networks that are invariant to photometric corruptions (ICLR 2026), anisotropy-aware training for particle picking (ISBI 2026), and realistic simulation of tomograms. I am currently building a universal particle picker that biologists can directly apply to their own data.

Before my PhD, I studied applied mathematics and computer science at CY Tech and AI at Paris Dauphine – PSL, and worked with Thomas Walter at Institut Curie on breast cancer histopathology. Outside of research, I row across the rivers of Europe.

I am looking for a new position, starting September 2027.
