About me

I am a PhD candidate in the SAIRPICO team at Inria and the University of Rennes, supervised by Charles Kervrann. My thesis develops deep learning methods to identify and localize macromolecules in 3D cryo-electron tomograms.

My interest in medical imaging started during my master's thesis, where I worked on quantifying breast cancer aggressiveness from IHC images and discussed the data with pathologists. I then pursued a PhD to develop my expertise in deep learning for biomedical image analysis. I particularly enjoy AI applied to the biomedical field and collaborating with specialists of the medical domain. I now want to work closer to clinicians and patients, on diagnosis and analysis.

My PhD defense is planned for April to May 2027, and I am looking for a position starting in September 2027.
